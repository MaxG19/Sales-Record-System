Inventory Settings

Configure how your system handles stock levels, SKUs, and warehouse alerts.

analytics

Track inventory

Enable global stock monitoring. If disabled, products will always show as "In Stock" regardless of physical quantity.

remove_shopping_cart

Allow negative stock

Permit sales to continue even when recorded stock reaches zero. Useful for pre-orders or drop-shipping models.

notification_important

Low stock alerts

Receive automated daily summaries and real-time dashboard notifications when item counts fall below safety thresholds.

barcode_scanner

Barcode required

Enforce mandatory barcode entry for all new stock items. Recommended for businesses utilizing warehouse scanners.

auto_fix_high

Auto-generate SKU

Automatically create internal Stock Keeping Units based on product category, brand, and sequence numbers.

content_copy

Allow duplicate SKU

Allow different products to share the same SKU identifier. Disabling this is highly recommended for inventory integrity.

info

Pro Tip: Inventory Integration

Changes to these settings may affect your synchronization with external marketplaces like Shopify or Amazon. Ensure your team is notified before altering stock tracking behaviors.
