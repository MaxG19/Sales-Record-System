Frequently Asked Questions

View all FAQs arrow_forward

How do I update my branch inventory?

expand_more

You can update branch inventory by navigating to the "Products" tab in your sidebar, selecting the specific product, and adjusting the stock levels for each location. Changes are synced in real-time across all dashboard views.

Where can I download my monthly sales report?

expand_more

Monthly reports are generated on the 1st of every month. Go to "Reports" > "Financials" and click the download icon next to the relevant month. Reports are available in PDF and CSV formats.

Can I manage multiple workspaces with one login?

expand_more

Yes, if you have enterprise permissions. You can switch between workspaces by clicking the "Workspace" header in the navigation drawer and selecting "Switch Account."

What should I do if a transaction is pending for too long?

expand_more

Pending transactions usually resolve within 15 minutes. If it exceeds 2 hours, please check your network connection or contact Technical Support using the "Live Chat" button above with the Transaction ID.
